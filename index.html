What it does

Find and remove duplicate rows

Highlight duplicates in colour before you delete anything, or remove them in one click. Match on every column, or only the ones that identify a row — SKU, email address, order number. Case is ignored by default, so A-101 and a-101 are correctly treated as the same row.

Trim spaces

Removes spaces at the start and end of cells and collapses double spaces. These are invisible, and they are the single most common reason a VLOOKUP or a filter silently returns nothing.

Delete empty rows and columns

Clears out the blank rows and columns that exports and copy-paste leave behind.

Fix capitalisation

Title Case, Sentence case, UPPERCASE or lowercase — across the whole sheet, or only the cells you've selected.

It backs up before it deletes

Ctrl+Z does not undo changes made by an add-on. Most people find that out the hard way, after something is already gone.

SheetTidy copies the tab before any destructive change, and tells you what the backup is called. It's on by default. You can turn it off, but you probably shouldn't.

It only ever sees the sheet you're in

Most cleanup add-ons ask for permission to “see, edit, create and delete all your Google Sheets™ spreadsheets” — or all your Drive files. SheetTidy asks for access to the one spreadsheet you have open, and nothing else.

Compare the permission screens before you install anything, including this. Your data never leaves Google. There is no server, nothing is uploaded, and no spreadsheet content is logged. The privacy policy spells out exactly what is and isn't stored.

Install from the Google Workspace Marketplace, open any spreadsheet, then Extensions → SheetTidy → Open SheetTidy. The panel appears on the right. There's no account to create and nothing to configure.
